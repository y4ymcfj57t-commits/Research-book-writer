# Claim Ledger

## Harry S. Truman: Slavery, Racism, and Civil Rights

This ledger documents all significant claims with supporting evidence, confidence levels, and verification status.

---

## Claim Type Key
- **F** = Fact (verifiable event, date, name, action)
- **I** = Interpretation (analysis, meaning, causation)
- **Q** = Quotation (direct words)
- **C** = Context (historical background)

## Confidence Level Key
- **V** = Verified (multiple independent sources)
- **P** = Probable (reliable source, no contradiction)
- **CT** = Contested (sources disagree)
- **SP** = Speculative (inference from evidence)

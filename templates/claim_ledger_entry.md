## Usage Guidelines

1. **When to create an entry:**
   - Every specific fact (dates, names, events, numbers)
   - Every interpretation or analysis
   - Every direct quotation
   - Every reconstructed scene
   - Any claim that could be challenged

2. **When a quick entry suffices:**
   - Routine, uncontroversial facts
   - Well-documented events
   - Claims with obvious, accessible sources

3. **When full entry is required:**
   - Sensitive or controversial claims
   - Claims central to thesis
   - Claims with counter-evidence
   - Claims involving living persons
   - Any claim rated SIGNIFICANT or higher

4. **Maintenance:**
   - Update entries as new evidence emerges
   - Update verification status after checking
   - Update after red team review
   - Keep revision history for significant changes
